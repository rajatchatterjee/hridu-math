# Hridu's Great Rescue

A fly-run-and-rescue adventure for an 8-year-old, with a **Puzzle Pit Stop**
every few minutes: one math problem and one brain puzzle to refuel Zippy the
plane before play continues.

- **Play in a browser (phone / laptop):** https://rajatchatterjee.github.io/hridu-math/
- **Google TV / Android TV APK:** https://github.com/rajatchatterjee/hridu-math/releases/latest/download/hridu-great-rescue.apk
  (install with the Downloader app on the TV)

Controls: arrows + OK on the TV remote or keyboard, Back/Esc to pause.
On phones, use the on-screen buttons.

Grown-ups can change how often puzzles appear (3, 5, 8 or 10 minutes) on the
title screen.
